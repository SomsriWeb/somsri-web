  ## How to order
  
  #description
  Four simple steps for a smooth, quality-backed order:
  
  1. **Send your concept** — After choosing fabric direction, share reference photos or sketches, logo files, print artwork, copy, and colors so our team understands the full vision before design starts.
  2. **Graphics mockups** — Our designers prepare shirt visuals with layout, logos, and details. You review and request revisions before production begins.
  3. **Production after approval** — Once approved, manufacturing starts under our QC standards, typically within about 15 days. Rush dates can be discussed in advance.
  4. **Delivery + 90-day satisfaction support** — Every piece is checked before shipping, packed safely, and sent to you. We stand behind workmanship issues (e.g. sizing, color drift, misprint) with support to fix or replace.

  ## Fabrics and features
  
  #description
  We stock many fabrics for different needs:
  
  - **Cotton waterproof** — Cotton feel inside with a water-resistant face for light liquid protection.
  - **Combed cotton** — Combed yarns for a softer, stronger tee or polo face.
  - **Semi-combed** — Mid-tier cotton between carded and combed — good for promos and staff tees.
  - **Dry feel** — Moves sweat to the outer surface for a drier, cooler feel — running and activewear.
  - **Dry tech** — Performance evolution of dry feel — odor control, antibacterial or UV options, lightweight builds for serious sport.
  - **Juti micro** — Fine microfiber, light yet dense, soft and breathable — uniforms and sportswear.
  - **Micro sport** — Sport-focused microfiber: quick-dry, stretch, durable — football, running, training.
  - **Muslin** — Natural, airy, soft — breathable casual layers.
  - **Nano waterproof** — Nano coating for water resistance without a stiff hand.
  - **Supersoft** — Extra-soft blends for premium hand-feel.
  - **TC Lacoste** — Polyester–cotton piqué look — smart polos and staff shirts.
  - **TC / TK** — TC blends for affordable, easy-care tees and polos; TK polyester knits for budget, durable uniforms.
