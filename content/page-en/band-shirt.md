FAQ — Custom Band Shirts

#title
The Most Common Questions Before Ordering Band Shirts

#item-1-question
What is the minimum order for band shirts?

#item-1-answer
The minimum order is just 100 pieces per design, and you can mix sizes as needed. If you need a smaller quantity, feel free to contact our team to discuss the details.

#item-2-question
How long does band shirt production take?

#item-2-answer
Standard production time is about 14–21 business days from the date the design is finalized and production is confirmed.

#item-3-question
What fabrics are available for band shirts?

#item-3-answer
Choose from more than 50 fabrics, such as Cotton 100%, Cotton Comb 32, vintage washed fabric (Faded Color), and tie-dye, to suit your band's style and budget.

#item-4-question
Can you make tie-dye band shirts?

#item-4-answer
Yes. Tie-dye adds 60 THB to the price of a standard T-shirt. Send us your design, pattern, and preferred color tones via LINE chat and our admin will give you a quote.

#item-5-question
Can I order band shirts online without visiting the factory?

#item-5-answer
Yes, 100% online. Send your artwork files, discuss fabrics, and approve samples via LINE chat. Our team takes care of every step and delivers nationwide.

#item-6-question
What does the 90-day warranty on band shirts cover?

#item-6-answer
Our 90-day quality warranty covers defects in sewing, printing, or screen printing, as well as any other production errors. If you find a problem, contact us and we will inspect and process your claim right away.
