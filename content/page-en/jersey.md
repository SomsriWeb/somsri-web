  #title
  Printing Technique (Sublimation)

  #description
  A highly popular printing method that uses heat to make the ink penetrate into the fabric. It is widely used for sportswear, polo shirts and jerseys.

  #features-title
  Advantages of Sublimation Printing

  #features
  - The print lies flat with the fabric — no thick, raised film layer on the shirt surface
  - No cracking or peeling like film-based prints, because the ink penetrates the fibers
  - Fully custom team designs — arrange colors, logos, lettering and graphics to match your design

  #title
  What Is a Jersey\
  and Why Is It the Top Choice\
  for Team Shirts and Sportswear?

  #description
  A jersey is a printed sports shirt made from micro sport fabric. It is breathable, lightweight and allows free movement, making it popular for sports team shirts, E-Sport shirts, event shirts and corporate uniforms that need to keep up with an active day.

  **The advantage of producing jerseys with sublimation**\
  is that the print penetrates the fabric, so colors stay vivid and sharp without peeling even after frequent washing. You can print the entire shirt without limits — ideal for custom jerseys with complex designs or many colors.

  The Questions People Ask Most Before Ordering Custom Jerseys

  #item-1-question
  What printing technique do jerseys use, and will the print come off after washing?

  #item-1-answer
  We use sublimation printing, where the ink penetrates the fabric directly. There is no film layer to crack or peel, so colors stay as sharp as ever after many washes.

  #item-2-question
  Will the jersey shrink or stretch out of shape?

  #item-2-answer
  No. It won't shrink or lose its shape easily, because we use quality micro sport fabric that holds its form wash after wash.

  #item-3-question

  #item-3-answer
  The minimum order is just 100 pieces — suitable for both small and large projects.

  #item-4-question
  I don't have a design file — can I still order custom jerseys?

  #item-4-answer
  Yes. Our team offers a free design service — just tell us your idea or the theme you want.

  #item-5-question
  How long does jersey production take?

  #item-5-answer
  Delivery is guaranteed within 90 days of design confirmation and payment.

  #item-6-question
  How is a jersey different from regular sportswear?

  #item-6-answer
