  What Is a Washed Shirt?
  :::

  #description
  ::::prose-p
  Faded washed shirts have been a legendary favorite across generations of vintage fashion lovers. They usually come in black and grey tones, in many shades such as jet black, faded black, reddish black, or greenish black. Brand-new shirts are given extra value by putting them through a washing process or bleach treatment, creating a unique look with faded color and subtle mottling, so the fabric resembles a well-worn garment — what we call a vintage look.
  :::

  :::prose-p
  Today these shirts are hugely popular because the prints look dimensional, cool, and one-of-a-kind. We use genuine premium-grade cotton at 20 Comb weight for great breathability, a fuller hand-feel, a soft and comfortable touch, good sweat absorption, and durability. Washed shirts are often ordered in an oversized fit — looser and larger than regular sizing — for a cooler, more stylish look that moves freely without feeling tight or restrictive.
  :::

  Care Tips
  :::
  #description
  **Wash separately from other fabrics**: Newly bought dyed garments may bleed color, so they should not be washed together with other fabrics.

  **Avoid strong detergents**: Harsh detergents can strip the color and fade the fabric faster, so use a mild detergent to keep your shirt looking its best.
