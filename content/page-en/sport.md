FAQ — Custom Sportswear

#title
The Questions Teams and Organizations Ask Most Before Ordering Sportswear

#item-1-question
What is the minimum order for custom sportswear?

#item-1-answer
The minimum is 20 pieces per design. For smaller quantities, chat with our team to discuss the details.

#item-2-question
How long does sportswear production take?

#item-2-answer
Delivery is guaranteed within 14–21 working days from sample approval.

#item-3-question
Can you produce team jerseys with player numbers and names?

#item-3-answer
Yes. We print numbers, player names and sponsor logos as required at no extra cost.

#item-4-question
I don't have a design — can Somsri design the sportswear for me?

#item-4-answer
Yes. Our artwork team will lay out and design it for free — just send us your logo or a rough idea.

#item-5-question
How is sublimation printing different from regular screen printing?

#item-5-answer
Sublimation dyes the ink directly into the fabric, so colors are sharp and won't peel or crack — even with full-body prints or gradients. Regular screen printing, by contrast, lays ink on top of the fabric surface.
