    ### Choosing Fabric Before You Order

    #description
    When choosing fabric, consider the brand concept you have in mind, because fabric affects both the image of your brand and the wearer's experience. For example, a concept focused on comfort and everyday wear suits cotton and Super Soft fabrics, which are soft and breathable. A sportswear concept suits micro sports fabric, while uniforms and staff shirts suit TC Lacoste fabric.
