### [Canvas](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NWYxjTwmygE)

A timeless favorite and the most popular fabric for tote bags. Canvas is 100% cotton, similar in look to calico or sackcloth, giving a simple, natural and classic look that never goes out of style and suits every outfit. It is strong, durable, holds weight well and is affordable. Canvas totes can be printed, screen printed or embroidered with names as you like, making them a special gift with a distinctive identity.

### [Spunbond](https://th.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E0%B8%AA%E0%B8%9B%E0%B8%B1%E0%B8%99%E0%B8%9A%E0%B8%AD%E0%B8%99%E0%B8%94%E0%B9%8C)

An attractive option for anyone looking for an easy-to-use or rugged bag. Spunbond is a synthetic fabric that is strong, durable, somewhat water-resistant and lightweight — which is why spunbond bags are more popular every day. Easy to carry anywhere and cheaper than other fabric bags, they are ideal as premium giveaways or event souvenirs.

### [Polyester](https://th.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E0%B8%9E%E0%B8%AD%E0%B8%A5%E0%B8%B4%E0%B9%80%E0%B8%AD%E0%B8%AA%E0%B9%80%E0%B8%97%E0%B8%AD%E0%B8%A3%E0%B9%8C)

Another very popular fabric, valued for its strength and resistance to tearing, so it lasts a long time. It is lightweight and easy to carry, dries quickly when wet without musty odors, holds its shape without frequent ironing, and comes in many colors and designs — suitable for all kinds of occasions, and best of all, it's affordable.

### [Calico (Unbleached Cotton)](https://www.mattcorner.com/what-is-%E0%B8%9C%E0%B9%89%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%94%E0%B8%B4%E0%B8%9A)

A beautiful fabric that remains consistently popular. Its unique texture gives calico bags a charm all their own. It is durable and versatile; the only limitation is that it comes in just one color — but you can screen print any design you like onto it.

Each fabric has different properties suited to different uses. If you have any questions, contact us on LINE: @somsritshirt — the Somsri team is always happy to help.

## Tote Bags as Premium Corporate Giveaways

Tote bags are no longer just containers for carrying things — they are hugely popular premium items to give to customers, employees and partners. Besides being genuinely useful, they promote your brand and leave a great impression on recipients. Choosing tote bags is eco-friendly, reducing the use of plastic bags — a major environmental problem — and shows you care about the environment and the recipient's wellbeing. They can be used on many occasions, such as going to the market, shopping or going to work. Most importantly, printing your company's logo or message on a tote bag helps your brand become better known. Just choose a well-designed, high-quality tote and it will impress recipients and keep your brand top of mind.
