FAQ: Custom Tote Bag Manufacturing

#title
The most common questions before ordering custom tote bags

#item-1-question
What is the minimum order for custom tote bags?

#item-1-answer
The minimum order is 150 bags per design. If you need fewer, message our team to discuss the details.

#item-2-question
What tote bag styles are available, and can the size be adjusted?

#item-2-answer
We offer 3 standard styles: Gusset, Korea and Square. Size, handle length and fabric color can be adjusted to your brief.

#item-3-question
Will the logo printing on custom tote bags peel off?

#item-3-answer
No. We use high-quality screen printing techniques with long-lasting colors, so prints stay sharp even after many washes.

#item-4-question
How long does tote bag production take?

#item-4-answer
Delivery is guaranteed within 90 days after the design and sample are approved.

#item-5-question
Can I order custom tote bags without a design file?

#item-5-answer
Yes. Our artwork team will prepare the layout and design for free. Just send your logo or a rough idea.
