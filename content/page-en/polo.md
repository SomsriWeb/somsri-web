FAQ: Custom Polo Shirt Manufacturing

#title
The most common questions teams and organizations ask before ordering polo shirts

#item-1-question
What is the minimum order for custom polo shirts?

#item-1-answer
The minimum order is 20 pieces per design. If you need fewer, message our team to discuss the details.

#item-2-question
How long does polo shirt production take?

#item-2-answer
Delivery is guaranteed within 14-21 business days from sample approval.

#item-3-question
Can you produce team polo shirts with player numbers and names?

#item-3-answer
Yes. We can print numbers, player names and sponsor logos as required, at no extra cost.

#item-4-question
I don't have a design. Can Somsri design the polo shirt for me?

#item-4-answer
Yes. Our artwork team will prepare the layout and design for free. Just send your logo or a rough idea.

#item-5-question
How is sublimation printing on polo shirts different from regular screen printing?

#item-5-answer
Sublimation infuses the ink directly into the fabric, giving sharp colors that won't peel or crack, even on all-over prints or color gradients. Regular screen printing, by contrast, lays ink on top of the fabric surface.
