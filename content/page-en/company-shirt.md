## Fabrics for Polo Shirts and Uniforms

[TC Lacoste](https://tsknitting.co.th/%E0%B8%9C%E0%B9%89%E0%B8%B23%E0%B8%8A%E0%B8%99%E0%B8%B4%E0%B8%94%E0%B8%AA%E0%B8%B3%E0%B8%AB%E0%B8%A3%E0%B8%B1%E0%B8%9A%E0%B8%9C%E0%B8%A5%E0%B8%B4%E0%B8%95%E0%B9%80%E0%B8%AA%E0%B8%B7%E0%B9%89%E0%B8%AD) is a very popular fabric for polo shirts, especially corporate polos, thanks to several standout properties. Polo shirts made from it are durable, comfortable and look great, with moderate breathability at an affordable price. It is well suited to year-to-year use — the color may fade gradually with wear, but the fabric stays just as soft and comfortable.

[Dry Tech](https://www.winnaargarment.com/content/6787/dry-tech-%E0%B8%9C%E0%B9%89%E0%B8%B2%E0%B9%81%E0%B8%AB%E0%B9%88%E0%B8%87%E0%B8%AD%E0%B8%99%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%84%E0%B8%95) is another very popular fabric for polo shirts and uniforms. Its special properties deliver both comfort and durability, giving employees a sharper, more professional image. It is lightweight, colorfast and pill-resistant, easy to iron and wicks sweat well — comfortable all day long, soft on the skin and ready for any situation.

[Micro Sport](/type-of-fabric) is ideal for active wear. With excellent breathability and sweat absorption, it is comfortable to wear and well suited to outdoor activities and exercise. Made from high-quality fibers, the fabric is soft, comfortable and non-irritating, and stands up well to washing, ironing and daily use. It is very easy to care for — just machine wash as normal — and it dries quickly, too.
