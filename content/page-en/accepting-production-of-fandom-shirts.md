## The Rise of Fandoms and Gifts for Artists

“[Fandom](https://www.sanook.com/campus/1416007/)” refers to a community of fans who come together to support artists, actors, idols, or other people they admire. Fandom culture goes back a long way, to the 60s and 70s, much like band T-shirts. The dazzling charm and distinctive personalities of artists have won over countless fans, giving rise to fandom culture and the tradition of giving gifts to artists — a way to make a lasting impression and be remembered. Fandom shirts tell the story of the strong relationship between artists and their fans, creating a bond that drives their huge popularity today.
