FAQ

#title
Frequently asked questions about custom corporate polo shirts

#item-1-question
What is the minimum order for custom polo shirts?

#item-1-answer
SomsriTshirt produces polo shirts from a minimum of 20 pieces per design, with mixed sizes allowed. The price table starts at 100 pieces; for 20–99 pieces, message us on LINE for a quote.

#item-2-question
How long does it take to produce custom corporate polo shirts?

#item-2-answer
New custom-made orders at SomsriTshirt take 14–21 business days after design approval. If you need them sooner, choose ready-made polo shirts with an embroidered logo, which take 2–3 days.

#item-3-question
How much does a custom polo shirt cost per piece?

#item-3-answer
SomsriTshirt blank polo shirts start at 120 THB per piece (Micro Sport/Micro Juti, 601–700 pieces) up to 220 THB per piece (Dry Tech/Dry Feel, 100–250 pieces), excluding embroidery, screen printing and 7% VAT. Embroidery is quoted based on the size and detail of the design. See the [price table by fabric and quantity](#price).

#item-4-question
Which fabric is best for corporate polo shirts?

#item-4-answer
For office uniforms, SomsriTshirt recommends TC Lacoste or Dry Feel. For outdoor work or restaurants, we recommend Dry Tech. All-over prints require 100% polyester fabric such as Micro Juti. [See the fabric comparison](#fabric)

#item-5-question
Can you issue a tax invoice to our company?

#item-5-answer
Yes. SomsriTshirt issues quotations and tax invoices in your company name.

#item-6-question
Can we see a sample before production?

#item-6-answer
Yes. SomsriTshirt makes a sample for your approval before mass production on every order, free of charge.

#item-7-question
Do you ship polo shirts outside Bangkok?

#item-7-answer
SomsriTshirt offers free delivery within Bangkok and ships nationwide via insured courier.

#item-8-question
How long is the product guarantee?

#item-8-answer
SomsriTshirt guarantees its production work for 90 days after delivery, covering manufacturing defects.
