  ## Hawaiian Shirt Fabric and Features
  :::

Our Hawaiian shirts are designed to keep the signature tropical look while being elegant and practical for everyday wear. We use Italian Silk, renowned for its soft, smooth and gracefully light drape. Italian silk feels cool and gentle on the skin, making it ideal for Thailand's hot, humid weather and tropical climates around the world.

The fine fibers and subtle sheen give each shirt a lively look from every angle, whether you are relaxing by the sea, enjoying a laid-back day or working in resort-style fashion. The fabric is breathable, quick-drying, resists mustiness and holds its shape well even after many washes. Printed colors stay crisp and resist fading, for a look that is both vibrant and luxurious.

An Italian silk Hawaiian shirt is more than an ordinary shirt. It is a blend of fashion, comfort and premium craftsmanship that feels as good to wear as it looks.
