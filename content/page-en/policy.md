# Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2, 2026

**[SomsriTShirt]** ("we", "us") values your privacy. This policy explains how our application (the "App") collects, uses and manages data from your Facebook account.

This policy applies to connecting/authorizing the App to access the necessary data via the **Facebook Graph API** in order to provide notification and message-tracking services for our Facebook Page.

## 1. Data We Collect
In order to send notifications to our internal systems (such as Zoho Cliq), our App accesses the following data via the Facebook Graph API:

1. **Message Metadata:** The status of messages (e.g., "unread" or "read but not yet replied").
2. **Message Content:** Used to create a brief summary for forwarding/notifying our internal team.

## 2. Purpose of Data Use
We access the above data for one purpose only, namely:

1. **Notifications:** Tracking pending messages on our Facebook Page in order to send notifications to our company's internal application/system, so that our team can reply to customers promptly and no message is missed.

## 3. Sharing Data with Third Parties
We send notification data only to **Zoho Cliq**, which is our company's internal communication platform.

We do **not** sell, exchange or transfer your message data to any external third party for marketing or advertising purposes.

## 4. Data Retention
Our system acts only as a pass-through (proxy) for sending notifications. We do not permanently store message content in our database beyond what is necessary to process the notification at that time.

## 5. User Rights and Data Deletion
You may disconnect the App or ask us to delete access data at any time by:

1. Going to your Facebook settings (Settings & Privacy > Apps and Websites) and removing/revoking our App's permissions.
2. Contacting us directly at **[Contact email]** to request deletion of the related data.
